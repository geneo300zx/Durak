Quick rules

36-card deck (6–A). Everyone gets 6 cards; the last card sets the trump suit (beats every other suit).

The attacker throws a card at the defender. Beat it with a higher card of the same suit, or any trump. Undefeated cards get taken into your hand.

Other players may pile on more cards of a rank already on the table (up to 6, or the defender's hand size). Once the defender has beaten everything and nobody adds more, cards go to the discard.

When the deck runs dry, keep playing — the first to empty their hand is safe. Last one holding cards is the durak.
